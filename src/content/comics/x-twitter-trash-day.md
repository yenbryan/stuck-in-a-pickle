The rubbish bin is a metaphor for the rebrand, not a finding that X legally abandoned the Twitter name. The preliminary injunction restricts Operation Bluebird's use of that name while the case continues; it is not a final judgment. The judge treated "Tweet" and the blue bird logo differently, finding that X had abandoned those rights.

The characters and dialogue are invented. The bird represents the old branding, not a claim that X won protection for the logo.
