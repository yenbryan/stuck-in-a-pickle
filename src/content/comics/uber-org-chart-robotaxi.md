The factual summary reflects reporting that Uber planned to cut about 3,300 roles while reducing management layers and reallocating spending across its businesses, including robotaxis. Uber said the restructuring would make the company simpler and create room to invest in its future; it did not say autonomous vehicles directly caused the layoffs. The transforming org chart, executive and dialogue are satirical inventions.
