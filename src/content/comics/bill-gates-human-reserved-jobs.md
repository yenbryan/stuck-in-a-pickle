The factual summary is based on the linked reporting. Gates proposed that some occupations be kept for people even where AI could do the work, drew the comparison to land set aside as a nature reserve, and named childcare and jury service as examples. He told Axios that in a very extreme form he could imagine 40% of jobs initially reserved this way — "but that's as high as I can get."

The interview, the CAPTCHA and all dialogue are satirical devices. Gates did not propose making anyone pass a bot check to qualify for work.
