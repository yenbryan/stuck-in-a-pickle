The glacier misunderstands the blanket; the researchers do not. Insulation can help keep outside heat away from ice. The Keep It Wool experiment compares wool covers with a synthetic cover on small test areas, rather than wrapping an entire glacier in a household blanket.

Summit Foundation reports promising early observations, with the data still requiring analysis. This is a local protective measure, not a solution to climate change or a substitute for reducing greenhouse gas emissions. The talking glacier, scientist and dialogue are fictional.
