The factual summary is based on NASA's account of the FALCON demonstration. The system matched spacecraft and orbital debris observed by Starling's cameras against an onboard catalog, used those objects as navigation references and improved orbit estimates for more than 200 objects. The directions and dialogue are satirical inventions.
